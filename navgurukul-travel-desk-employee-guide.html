Profile & verification

Your profile gates your ability to book. Complete it first — everything downstream is faster for it.

Open Profile in the sidebar. Your completeness score is built from 11 fields, each worth roughly 9% (100 ÷ 11). Your email address is deliberately excluded, since it's fixed to your account.

The two source guides counted these fields slightly differently — one treated the two document uploads as separate items, the other bundled them and counted emergency contact relation instead. Emergency contact relation is collected, but on the travel request form (Step 3), not as part of the 11-field profile score.

Document verification lifecycle

Both uploads move through the same four states:

Incomplete Pending Verification Approved or Rejected

A rejected document must be re-uploaded, which puts it back into the Pending Verification queue.

The temporary unlock window

If your organisation enforces verification before booking, you aren't left waiting on the PNC review queue. Uploading your documents — or clicking Skip for Now on the dashboard prompt — grants a time-boxed grace period.

Default window

7 days

Admin-configurable, 1–30 days

Urgent warning at

≤ 2 days

Countdown turns amber

If rejected

No grace

Locked out immediately

  • While the window is active you'll see a countdown: "You have X days remaining to use the travel desk while your documents are reviewed."
  • At 2 days or fewer, that message escalates to an amber warning.
  • If the window expires before approval, you're locked out of submitting new requests until an Admin or PNC user approves your documents.
  • If a document is rejected, there's no grace period at all — you're locked immediately and must re-upload.
  • Once approved, the countdown disappears permanently.
Bottom line

Upload documents on day one, not the day you need to fly. If your window is running down, chase PNC directly rather than waiting.

Policy violation check

Each travel mode carries a minimum advance-notice period set by PNC/Admin under Policies (flights, for example, are typically 14 days). The check runs the moment you've picked a mode and a departure date.

If you're inside that window, a "Policy Violation Detected" card appears and you must type a justification — e.g. "Client scheduled urgent meeting" — before you can continue to Step 3. Your reason is shown verbatim to your manager, who must then explicitly approve before PNC can book.

Separately, a Policy Violation badge appears on any request that was flagged for short notice, regardless of its current status.

Booked → Closed is automatic

A Booked request flips to Closed the moment PNC has entered ticket cost, vendor name, and uploaded a ticket or invoice. You don't need to do anything. If it's sat on Booked for a while, one of those three — usually the file upload — is still outstanding on PNC's side.

07

Responding to an On Hold request

PNC puts a request On Hold when they need something from you — "The morning flight is sold out, is afternoon acceptable?" or "Share your middle name exactly as on your Aadhaar."

  1. Your ticket card shows a warning strip: Action Required: Information Requested.
  2. Click the card to open the Request Details overlay.
  3. Read PNC's query under the PNC Clarification Needed block.
  4. Type your answer in the Your Response box.
  5. Click Submit Response & Resume Processing.

That single click clears the hold and returns the request to Processing, so PNC can carry on booking without another round trip.

Respond quickly

A held request isn't being worked on. Fares move, and seats you were quoted may be gone by the time you reply. Treat an On Hold notice as same-day.

08

Edit & resubmit after a rejection

Rejections aren't dead ends. Whether it came from your manager or PNC, you can correct and send it back through.

  1. Open the request. The rejection reason is stated in the detail view — e.g. "Wrong travel dates" or "Please select Train instead of Flight."
  2. Click Edit & Resubmit. The wizard reopens pre-filled with everything you entered before.
  3. Fix the flagged details and submit.
Three attempts, then it locks

You can resubmit a request a maximum of 3 times. On the fourth rejection the button is replaced with: "Resubmission cap reached. Please contact PNC or your manager directly." Resolve it manually from there.

Resubmission re-runs the check

A resubmitted request always returns to Not Started and the violation check runs again from scratch. This is deliberate: if your edit fixed the dates, it skips manager approval entirely; if it still violates policy, it correctly routes for approval again.

09

Cancellations & reconciliation

What happens when you cancel depends entirely on whether a ticket has already been bought.

Before booking

From Not Started, Approval Pending, Processing or On Hold, open the request and click Cancel Request. Type a reason — it's mandatory, you can't confirm without one — then click Confirm Cancellation.

The request moves straight to Cancelled by Employee with no penalty and no financial impact. Your reason is stored and shown back to you in the detail panel.

After booking

  1. Open the request and click Cancel Request.
  2. Confirm. Status becomes Cancellation Requested and it enters PNC's queue.
  3. PNC processes the cancellation with the vendor and records the refund percentage.

Who bears the cost?

CauseResolved asWho pays
Airline cancelled, ops change, price error Cancelled by PNC The organisation covers 100% of the loss. Nothing owed by you.
Your plans changed Cancelled by Employee You may owe a share of the non-refundable fare or penalty, split per the system cancellation policy.
Cancellations dashboard

Track refunds, org-absorbed costs and any balance you owe under Cancellations in the sidebar. A personal-reason balance stays open as Pending Refund until it's settled, typically via a salary adjustment.

Approving requests as a manager

If a colleague names you as their Approving Manager and their request trips a policy violation, it lands in your inbox.

The Approvals link appears in your sidebar with a count badge. Open it to see a card grid of pending requests; click a card to open the review modal, which shows:

  • The traveller's name, department, campus and email
  • Route, date, mode and trip type
  • Their stated purpose of travel
  • The specific policy violation and the justification they wrote

You then choose Reject or Approve Request.

Rejections need a reason

If you reject, you'll be prompted for a reason. It's stored and shown to the employee in their view of the request, so write something they can act on — "Take the train, it's a 6-hour route" beats "Not approved."

Once you action a request, if it was your last pending approval you're returned to your dashboard automatically.

11

Igatpuri meetup travel

The Igatpuri campus is the central hub for team meetups, workshops and offsites — travel to it has a shortcut.

If your department head or coordinator adds you to a finalised Igatpuri meetup list, a green notification banner appears on your dashboard. Click Book Travel on that banner and the wizard opens with your dates and purpose already filled in — you only need to supply your route and confirm your details.

Availability requests

Where the module is enabled, you may first submit your availability, which goes to the meetup approvers. Once approved, you can proceed with the travel booking. For questions about meetup logistics or coordination, reach the PNC team on Slack rather than through the travel desk.

PNC support chat Beta

Message the PNC team directly from inside the app, if your organisation has it enabled.

  1. Go to Chat Support in the sidebar.
  2. Click Start New Chat.
  3. Choose a thread type:
    • Existing Request — pick a submitted request from the dropdown; the conversation is linked to that ticket so PNC has full context.
    • Future Request — a trip you haven't submitted yet.
    • Others — general travel desk queries, reimbursement policy, feedback.
  4. Send your message. PNC admins see it in their support dashboard and reply.

Messages exchange in real time, and you can attach images or documents — visa copies, permission letters — directly in the chat window.

It's beta, so treat it as beta

Expect occasional bugs and delays. Don't rely on chat for anything urgent or time-sensitive — for those, contact PNC directly.

Honest limitations, so nothing catches you out.

FAQ

The questions PNC gets most often.

My request still says "Not Started" — is something broken?

No. That's the initial state while the system runs the policy check. It should move quickly to either Approval Pending (violation found) or Processing (clean).

It's been in Approval Pending for days. What now?

There's no automatic reminder or escalation for manager approvals yet. Follow up with your manager directly. If they're unavailable, contact PNC — they can advise on a fallback approver.

It's Booked but hasn't turned Closed. Do I need to do something?

No. It auto-closes once PNC has entered ticket cost, vendor name, and uploaded the ticket file. If it's lingering, one of those three is still missing on PNC's side — usually the file.

Can I edit a request after submitting?

If it's been Rejected by Manager or Rejected by PNC, yes — use Edit & Resubmit, up to 3 times.

For a request still in progress there's no live-edit option. Cancel it and submit a corrected one, or use chat for a minor clarification.

I don't know my exact departure time yet.

Choose Anytime in the Preferred Time dropdown. It exists for precisely this case and gives PNC the widest search.

My temporary unlock window is about to expire.

Contact PNC or an Admin and ask them to review your documents. Once the window lapses you can't submit new requests, though existing ones continue to be processed normally.

My document was rejected. Do I get a grace period?

No. Rejection locks booking immediately, with no temporary unlock. Re-upload the document and it re-enters the Pending Verification queue.

Will I owe money if I cancel?

Not if you cancel before the ticket is booked — that's clean, with no penalty.

After booking, it depends on cause. Airline or ops-driven cancellations are absorbed entirely by the organisation. Personal changes of plan may leave you liable for a share of the non-refundable fare, shown in the Cancellations dashboard as Pending Refund until settled.

I've hit the resubmission cap. What now?

The Edit & Resubmit button locks after 3 attempts. Contact PNC or your manager directly to resolve the underlying issue, then submit a fresh request.
